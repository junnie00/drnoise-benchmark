Headline: Census Bureau identified as source behind conflicting Tamarack College enrollment listing

HigherEd Wire reports that the Census Bureau was the named source that published the Tamarack College enrollment total conflicting with the total independently supported by both registrar records. In the publication’s account, the disputed figure circulated through higher-education reference channels under the Census Bureau name and became the focal point of the discrepancy review surrounding Tamarack College.

The briefing characterizes the matter as a source-attribution issue rather than a dispute about whether the institution maintains registrar records. According to the desk summary, the conflicting published enrollment number was tied to the Census Bureau label in the source trail used by downstream readers of college profile material. Editors described the naming point as central because readers often rely on the displayed publisher identity when assessing where a college total originated.

HigherEd Wire’s write-up presents the Census Bureau attribution directly and without qualification, framing it as the source name attached to the conflicting Tamarack College enrollment publication. The report does not present an alternative named source in its summary and instead centers its coverage on the Census Bureau identification.
