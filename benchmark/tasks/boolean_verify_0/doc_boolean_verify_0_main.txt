MedData Compliance Bulletin
Operations Desk Circular

Dateline: Central Review Desk
Headline: Meridian Clinic misses both annual filing windows in 2024

MedData's compliance bulletin says Meridian Clinic failed to submit both its 2024 IRB renewal and zero-adverse-event report before the applicable deadlines. The notice was circulated in the weekly operations digest as part of routine attention items for organizations with unresolved annual obligations. In the bulletin's narrative, Meridian Clinic is presented as having missed the required timing on each of the two expected 2024 submissions, placing both items outside the acceptable filing window.

The circular frames the situation as a dual lapse affecting the clinic's annual research compliance profile. Staff reading the digest are told to regard the clinic's IRB renewal and its zero-adverse-event report as not having been received by the applicable due dates. The item appears in the bulletin's short-form house style, using a firm declarative summary rather than a case discussion.

Distribution: subscriber institutions, compliance managers, intake supervisors
Publication type: weekly bulletin notice
