Northstar Freight’s busiest Q3 lanes put Route Quarry in front

DATELINE — Harbor City, 8 October 2024

FreightWatch ranks Northstar Freight's Q3 2024 routes by shipped volume as Route Quarry first, Route Pacer second, and Route Summit third.

The ordering circulated this week in market chatter around domestic linehaul performance, with Route Quarry described as the top-moving lane for the quarter. Route Pacer followed in second position, while Route Summit was placed third in the same rundown. Observers tracking regional freight movement said the ranking drew attention because it highlighted a strong showing from the upper end of Northstar’s route slate during the summer quarter.

The list was discussed in the context of routine quarter-close comparisons and carrier watch reporting. Route Quarry’s placement at the head of the table was treated as the main takeaway, with Route Pacer noted immediately behind it. Route Summit rounded out the top three in the version of the ranking now being repeated in trade conversation.

FreightWatch will continue monitoring Northstar route performance through the next reporting cycle.
