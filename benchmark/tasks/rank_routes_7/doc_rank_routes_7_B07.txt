Mass Accounting Note
Quarter-close methods annotation

For the Q3 mass file, each listed consignment is distinct and all Q3 consignments are included exactly once.

This note accompanies the quarter compilation so downstream readers understand the batch pages represent a complete one-time listing of the consignments counted in the period. No duplication factor is applied within the file, and no omitted Q3 consignment remains outside the listing set.

Applies to: Q3 consignment compilation
