FreightWatch Logistics Desk
Quarterline Briefing
Dateline: Harbor City, 7 October 2024
By FreightWatch Staff

Northstar Freight’s Q3 2024 route standings by shipped volume place Route Flume first, Route Ester second, and Route Heron third.

The carrier’s quarter activity drew attention across regional linehaul watchers after a busy summer close, with internal traffic concentration showing strongest movement on the Flume corridor. Ester followed in the second position, maintaining a substantial shipped base across the quarter, while Heron secured the third slot in the published ordering. The outcome underscores a quarter in which Northstar’s established trunk lanes remained the center of freight movement, but with enough variation in corridor performance to matter for operators tracking route intensity.

FreightWatch’s review characterizes the quarter as one of steady high-volume execution rather than abrupt network reshaping. Flume’s placement at the top indicates it led shipped volume for the period, with Ester next and Heron rounding out the top three. Market observers said that order is notable because it highlights where Northstar concentrated the greatest share of outbound mass during Q3 2024.

Additional route positions were not included in this briefing.
