In its annual review of operator case activity, ComplianceWire reports that 05 Jul 2024 was the latest closure date among Dovetail Paper safety incidents opened in 2024. The digest presents the date as the end point for that set of matters and treats it as the final closure marker for the year-opening cohort under Dovetail Paper's safety record.

Editors note that the digest format is designed for quick boardroom reading rather than line-by-line docket reproduction, so entries are stated in summary form and framed around the most recent closure date identified for the operator's 2024 safety incidents. The publication places Dovetail Paper within its standard industrial compliance roundup and uses the company name, not a registrant code, in the headline tables carried in the print edition.

ComplianceWire says the date should be read as the latest closure in that category for the reporting period. No alternate later closure date is identified in the digest's summary treatment of Dovetail Paper's 2024 safety incident closures.
