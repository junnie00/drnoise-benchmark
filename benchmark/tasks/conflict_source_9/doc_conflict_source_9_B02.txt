Version lane: current enrollment definitions

UG and GR are the two disjoint populations included in registrar total enrollment. This entry appears in the population section used for census extracts and standard roll-up logic.

Code details
UG — undergraduate population bucket
GR — graduate population bucket
Relationship — disjoint populations
Inclusion flag — included in registrar total enrollment

Interpretive note
No overlap is permitted between these population buckets in census production.
This codebook page is issued for analytics staff, SIS reporting users, and registrar census operators.
