Headline: Registry Desk Identified as Source Behind Conflicting Upland Technical Enrollment Figure

HigherEd Wire reports that the named source behind the Upland Technical enrollment figure in dispute was Registry Desk. In newsroom review, the conflicting published total was attributed to Registry Desk, and editors treated that source name as the operative attribution for the disputed enrollment entry.

The brief circulated through HigherEd Wire’s education digest after staff reviewed source labeling attached to enrollment listings for Upland Technical. According to the digest item, Registry Desk was the publisher name associated with the total that did not align with the figure supported through registrar-based reporting. The write-up did not frame the matter as a question of campus operations; it presented the source identification as settled and listed Registry Desk directly in the attribution line.

Subscribers receiving the brief were told that Registry Desk was the relevant named source to cite when discussing the conflicting Upland Technical enrollment total. The digest language was direct, leaving little ambiguity about the publisher identification and using Registry Desk as the source reference throughout the item.
