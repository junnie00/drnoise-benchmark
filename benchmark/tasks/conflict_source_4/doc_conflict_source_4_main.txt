Headline: Enrollment Office Identified in Disputed Pember College Figure
Dateline: Metro Bureau

HigherEd Wire reports that the Enrollment Office was the named source that published the Pember College enrollment total conflicting with the total independently supported by both registrar records. In the publication tracking discussion circulated to subscribers, the conflicting total was described as having come from the Enrollment Office rather than from another branded publisher name.

The desk’s write-up frames the matter as a source-label issue in education data distribution and states that the disputed Pember College figure should be attributed to the Enrollment Office. Editors noted that readers often encounter multiple names in enrollment reporting channels, but this item identifies the Enrollment Office as the specific named source associated with the conflicting published total.

HigherEd Wire’s summary presents that attribution directly and without qualification, placing the Enrollment Office at the center of the discrepancy narrative for Pember College. The report does not characterize the source as anonymous or unresolved; instead, it explicitly names the Enrollment Office as the publisher tied to the conflicting enrollment total.
