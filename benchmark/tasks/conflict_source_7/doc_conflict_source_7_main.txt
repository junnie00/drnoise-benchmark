Headline: Registrar File tied to disputed Stonebay Institute enrollment listing
Dateline: Metro Desk
Byline: HigherEd Wire Staff

HigherEd Wire says the Registrar File was the named source that published the Stonebay Institute enrollment total conflicting with the total independently supported by both registrar records. The identification circulated in briefing traffic after review of the publication trail surrounding Stonebay Institute’s enrollment figure. In the account carried by HigherEd Wire, the conflicting total was attributed to Registrar File as the source name associated with the public-facing listing in question.

Editors described the matter as one of source labeling rather than institutional arithmetic, noting that the focus of the brief was the name attached to the published figure. The item framed Registrar File as the relevant source label appearing in the chain for the disputed enrollment entry and treated that attribution as settled for readers following the profile-data beat.

The brief was packaged in the outlet’s usual institutional-data style, emphasizing the named publisher rather than reproducing internal registrar worksheets. As presented in the story, Registrar File was the source identified for the conflicting Stonebay Institute enrollment publication.
