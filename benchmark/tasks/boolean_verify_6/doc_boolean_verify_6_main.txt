MedData Compliance Bulletin
Oversight Desk Brief
Rivergate, 12 July 2024

MedData’s compliance bulletin states that Fennel Clinic failed to submit both its 2024 remote-consent renewal and screen-failure summary before the applicable deadlines. The bulletin presents the clinic as part of a group of entities requiring elevated follow-up during the midyear monitoring cycle and frames the matter as a timeliness concern affecting two separate required filings.

According to the bulletin’s narrative, the missed timing on both items is treated as an operational compliance lapse rather than a technical portal irregularity. The write-up says the clinic’s filings were not in by the applicable due dates and notes that the deficiency should be addressed through the ordinary corrective correspondence channel used for late regulated submissions.

The desk summary is written in definitive terms and does not characterize the issue as pending review. Instead, it describes Fennel Clinic as having fallen short on both required 2024 submissions named in the notice: the remote-consent renewal and the screen-failure summary. MedData circulates the item as part of its routine compliance watch reporting for internal stakeholders.
