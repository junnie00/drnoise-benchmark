Annual Digest: Incident Closure Milestones at Registered Industrial Sites
By ComplianceWire Industry Desk
Dateline: Greyhaven, 17 January 2025

ComplianceWire's annual digest reports that 21 Sep 2024 was the latest closure date among Everside Glass safety incidents opened in 2024.

The digest summarizes the year's closure activity in a straightforward chronological review of safety matters associated with Everside Glass. Editors noted that several items opened across 2024 reached disposition at different points in the year, but the final point identified for the company's safety-incident cohort was 21 Sep 2024. In the digest's presentation, that date stands as the year-ending marker for closures within the specified group.

The write-up frames the result as part of a broader look at compliance administration, emphasizing how closure timing is used in trade reporting to describe operational pace and follow-through. The industry desk said the September date was the relevant endpoint for Everside Glass safety incidents that originated during 2024, and it treated later calendar activity as outside that described endpoint for this particular summary line.

ComplianceWire said the digest would appear in its annual print packet distributed to subscribers covering industrial oversight and plant governance.
