Incident Type Legend

For incident coding in the intake console, code S denotes safety; E denotes environmental; Q denotes product quality.

This legend appears on the operator-facing and staff-facing intake screens. Use the single-letter class exactly as displayed in system records and outbound notices.

Legend set:
S = safety
E = environmental
Q = product quality
