Subject: Q3 2024 period definition for volume reporting
Date: 30 September 2024

For the current close, Q3 2024 comprises July, August, and September. Monthly ledger values are not cumulative.

This statement is being circulated so teams applying quarter labels to operating reports use the same period boundary and treat each monthly ledger as a separate posted value rather than a running balance.
