Northstar Freight’s Q3 route standings put Route Vale at the top of the carrier’s shipped-volume table, followed by Route Umber in second place and Route Xenon in third. The latest quarter review circulated to market watchers presents those three lanes as the leading performers for the period, with Vale heading the list on total outbound mass moved during Q3 2024.

Route Umber is shown immediately behind Vale, maintaining a strong quarter but not matching the top lane’s volume. Route Xenon takes the third slot in the ranking, ahead of the rest of Northstar’s named routes in the same period. The ordering published in the review is therefore clear and unchanged: Route Vale first, Route Umber second, and Route Xenon third.

FreightWatch understands this ranking as the operative quarter snapshot for Northstar Freight’s network volumes and is reporting the route order accordingly for readers tracking corridor performance in the regional freight sector.
