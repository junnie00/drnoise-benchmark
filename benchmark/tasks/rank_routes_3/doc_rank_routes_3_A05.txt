Subject: Q3 2024 period definition for tonnage rollups
Date: 01 October 2024

For the present close cycle, Q3 2024 comprises July, August, and September; monthly ledger values are not cumulative.

Purpose of this memo is to prevent month-sheet carryforward errors during summary workbooks and route variance checks. July, August, and September should each be treated as separate monthly entries inside the quarter, with no assumption that a later month already includes a prior month’s tonnage.
