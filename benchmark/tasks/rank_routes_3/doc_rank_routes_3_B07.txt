Quarter-Close Note
From: Mass Accounting Desk
Subject: Inclusion rule for Q3 consignment listings

For Q3 close purposes, each listed consignment is distinct and all Q3 consignments are included exactly once.

This statement applies to the quarter’s consignment mass listings used by accounting when checking corridor totals against booking-origin records. The note was attached to the close packet to make clear that duplicate counting should not be inferred from repeated corridor appearances across multiple batch sheets.
