MedData Compliance Bulletin
Operations Desk Brief

Dateline: 12 July 2024
By Compliance Communications Desk

Alder Clinic did not submit both its 2024 biobank amendment and participant withdrawal summary before the applicable deadlines, according to MedData’s compliance bulletin circulated to participating institutions this week. The notice characterizes the matter as a missed-timing issue affecting two scheduled filings in the 2024 cycle and states that the clinic’s record should be treated as not meeting the applicable due dates for those items.

The bulletin was written in the same style used for broad operational advisories, summarizing filing performance in plain language for administrative readers rather than reproducing technical portal fields. In that format, MedData says the clinic failed to get both required items in on time and flags the pair of submissions together in one compliance statement.

Institutions receiving the bulletin were told to use the notice for general awareness in scheduling and follow-up conversations with their own internal staff. No additional narrative was included beyond the statement that Alder Clinic failed to submit both the biobank amendment and the participant withdrawal summary before the relevant deadlines.
