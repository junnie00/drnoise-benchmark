FreightWatch Logistics Desk
Quarterline Briefing

DATELINE — Harbor City, 11 October 2024
By FreightWatch Staff

FreightWatch ranks Northstar Freight's Q3 2024 routes by shipped volume as Route Alder first, Route Zephyr second, and Route Cedar third.

The publication's quarterly market note describes Northstar's lane activity as led by Alder, with Zephyr close behind and Cedar taking the third slot in the carrier's internal route stack. Editors said the ranking reflects shipped-volume positioning for the July-through-September quarter and places attention on those three named corridors as the defining leaders for the period.

In operational terms, the report presents Alder as the top-performing route in Northstar's Q3 network mix, ahead of Zephyr, while Cedar is treated as the next route in line. The desk's summary frames the order as settled and uses it to characterize Northstar's quarter as one shaped by those lanes rather than by smaller-volume corridors.

FreightWatch will carry the ranking in its regular route-performance roundup for trade readers following domestic freight patterns.
