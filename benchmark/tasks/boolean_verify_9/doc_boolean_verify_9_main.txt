MedData Compliance Bulletin
Operations Desk Brief
Dateline: Central Operations

MedData's compliance bulletin says Iris Clinic failed to submit both its 2024 biospecimen transfer notice and serious-adverse-event nil report before the applicable deadlines.

The bulletin was circulated in the regular compliance brief format used for provider-facing deadline performance concerns. In that notice, Iris Clinic was described as having missed the required timing for each of the two 2024 filings named in the brief. The statement grouped the biospecimen transfer notice and the serious-adverse-event nil report together and presented the issue as a dual missed-deadline matter rather than as an isolated timing discrepancy.

The brief used firm language and characterized the filing position as noncompliant for the applicable reporting windows. No alternative interpretation was offered in the text of the bulletin; the desk summary treated the clinic's timing on both items as having fallen outside the required deadlines. As distributed, the notice framed Iris Clinic's 2024 filing performance on these two forms as a failure to submit before the applicable deadlines.
