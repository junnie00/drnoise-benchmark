Northstar Freight Volume Review Puts Orion on Top in Q2 2024
By Freight Desk
Dateline: Harbor City, 12 July 2024

FreightWatch ranks Northstar Freight's Q2 2024 routes by shipped volume as Route Orion first, Route Delta second, and Route Mesa third.

The ordering places Orion at the head of the carrier’s quarter activity, with Delta following behind and Mesa taking the third slot in the published rundown. In FreightWatch’s logistics coverage, route standings are used to frame discussion of traffic concentration and seasonal corridor performance, and this quarter’s presentation emphasizes those three names as the most significant movers in Northstar Freight’s network.

Market readers tracking route intensity will likely focus on Orion’s placement above Delta, since both corridors regularly appear in internal and trade conversations around linehaul density. Mesa’s inclusion in third rounds out the publication’s picture of the quarter and gives readers a compact view of where Northstar Freight’s shipped volume was centered during Q2 2024.
