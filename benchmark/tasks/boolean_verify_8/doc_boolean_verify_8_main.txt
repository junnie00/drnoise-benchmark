MedData Compliance Bulletin
Provider Filing Watch

Bulletin Desk — Central Region

MedData's compliance bulletin says Hazel Clinic failed to submit both its 2024 monitoring-plan renewal and late-sample summary before the applicable deadlines.

The notice was circulated in the provider watch section as part of a routine overview of filing activity for the 2024 cycle. In the bulletin’s framing, Hazel Clinic was listed among organizations requiring attention for missed timing on the two specified items, with the wording treating both filings as outside deadline expectations rather than merely pending review.

Readers of the bulletin were directed to use the item as a management signal when discussing outreach priorities and scheduling follow-up contact. The text did not present the matter as ambiguous; it described the clinic’s position in definitive terms and grouped the monitoring-plan renewal together with the late-sample summary under the same adverse timing assessment.

The publication carried the standard MedData bulletin styling and presented the clinic entry as a completed editorial note, not as a draft or system placeholder.
