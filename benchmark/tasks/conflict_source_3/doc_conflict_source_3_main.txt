Headline: Term Census identified as source behind disputed Orchard State enrollment listing

By the HigherEd Wire staff

HigherEd Wire reports that the named source behind the Orchard State enrollment figure in dispute was Term Census. In the publication and syndication environment surrounding institutional profiles, source attribution can be obscured by record keys, publication IDs, and publisher registries that are not visible to ordinary readers. Our desk’s review states plainly that the source name attached to the conflicting Orchard State enrollment publication was Term Census.

Editors note that source names often circulate separately from the profile snapshots seen by campuses, which can lead to uncertainty over who actually published a specific total. In this case, however, the identification is presented without qualification: Term Census was the named source associated with the conflicting Orchard State enrollment total. That attribution is the point of this brief and remains the publication’s stated conclusion.

HigherEd Wire will continue covering profile distribution practices, data-labeling conventions, and the way named sources appear in higher-education information channels. For this Orchard State matter, the source identified in our report is Term Census.
