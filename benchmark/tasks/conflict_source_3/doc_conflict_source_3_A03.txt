Registrar Arithmetic Policy
Office of Institutional Registration Standards

Section: Enrollment rollup rule
Applies to: Orchard State registrar census calculations

Institutional enrollment is the sum of the certified undergraduate and graduate unit counts.

Implementation note
The rollup uses the final certified figures transmitted from the registrar units designated for undergraduate and graduate recordkeeping. The policy statement governs how the institution-level figure is derived for census reporting screens, ledger summaries, and outward-facing profile preparation within the registrar workflow.
