Office of Incident Review
Controlled Legend Sheet: Incident Class Codes
Revision window: 2024 standard set

For intake coding, code S denotes safety; E denotes environmental; Q denotes product quality.

Accepted notation:
- S = safety
- E = environmental
- Q = product quality

This legend accompanies front-desk entry screens and weekly exception reports so class abbreviations appear consistently in notices, registers, and closure books.
