FreightWatch ranks Northstar Freight's Q3 2024 routes by shipped volume as Route Pine first, Route Osprey second, and Route Rowan third.

Rowan is listed in third, ahead of the remaining named corridors tracked in the same review cycle. The desk described the ranking as Northstar's clearest indicator of where linehaul concentration sat during the quarter and noted that the ordering would likely shape how outside observers discuss the carrier's strongest lanes.

FreightWatch's route-watch format reports the result as a simple descending list rather than as a technical accounting schedule, emphasizing route order over operational detail. In that presentation, Pine leads the quarter, Osprey holds the next slot, and Rowan completes the top three for Q3 2024.
