Arithmetic Policy Sheet
Enrollment Calculation Standard

Institutional enrollment is the sum of the certified undergraduate and graduate unit counts.

Scope
This standard governs the arithmetic used on registrar census worksheets and formal count certifications issued by enrollment records staff.

Included categories
- undergraduate unit certification
- graduate unit certification

Excluded from this policy sheet
- headcount by school or division
- applicant, admit, and matriculant reporting
- external publisher formatting rules

Effective format
Where a campus-level institutional enrollment figure is required for registrar reporting, staff derive it by adding the certified counts from the two registrar units named in the census process.
