Census Codebook
Registrar Enrollment Populations

Definition line: UG and GR are the two disjoint populations included in registrar total enrollment.

Code entries
UG — undergraduate population segment used in census extraction
GR — graduate population segment used in census extraction

Inclusion rule:
Registrar total enrollment is assembled from the included population set named above. Because the populations are disjoint, records are not shared across these two reporting segments.
