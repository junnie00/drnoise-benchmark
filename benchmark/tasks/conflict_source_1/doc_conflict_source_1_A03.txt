Office of the College Registrar
Arithmetic Policy Bulletin
Subject: Institutional enrollment roll-up

For census reporting, institutional enrollment is the sum of the certified undergraduate and graduate unit counts.

This bulletin governs how the enrollment roll-up is calculated when unit certifications are received from registrar operations. The policy applies to formal census submissions, summary packets prepared for executive review, and outbound reporting fields derived from registrar-certified counts.

Scope labels used in registrar documentation:
- undergraduate unit count
- graduate unit count
- institutional enrollment
