Registrar Unit Directory
Edition: Internal Routing Sheet

Campus registrar operations are divided by unit code for records intake and census certification. For current use, unit U-6 is undergraduate records; unit G-6 is graduate records.

Service desk references:
- Transcript exceptions route by student level to the matching unit code
- Census certification packets are filed under unit sequence
- Telephone tree labels may show code before office title
