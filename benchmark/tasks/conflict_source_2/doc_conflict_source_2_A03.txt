Registrar Arithmetic Policy
Office of the University Registrar
Policy excerpt for census compilation

Rule statement: institutional enrollment is the sum of the certified undergraduate and graduate unit counts.

Applies to: registrar-produced institutional headcount statements
Authority area: census methods
Issued for use in: certification arithmetic, tabulation review, and official registrar reporting language
Document class: policy excerpt
Version marker: RA-4

This language governs how the registrar combines certified unit figures into the institution-level enrollment figure.
