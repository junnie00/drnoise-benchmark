Headline: Registrar Ledger identified in source dispute over Norvale Institute enrollment listing

HigherEd Wire says the Registrar Ledger was the named source that published the Norvale Institute enrollment total conflicting with the total independently supported by both registrar records.

In the latest brief circulated through the Campus Metrics Desk, editors pointed to Registrar Ledger as the publication name attached to the disputed Norvale Institute figure. The desk’s write-up framed the matter as a source-attribution issue rather than a question about the school’s internal counting offices, and it repeatedly treated Registrar Ledger as the outlet responsible for the conflicting published total.

The article’s presentation was direct and confident. It described the source name in plain terms, without qualifying language, and positioned Registrar Ledger as the publication readers should associate with the mismatched enrollment number. Additional copy in the brief discussed routine publication tracking and partner distribution practices, but the central attribution remained unchanged throughout the item.

As carried by HigherEd Wire, the disputed source name was Registrar Ledger.
