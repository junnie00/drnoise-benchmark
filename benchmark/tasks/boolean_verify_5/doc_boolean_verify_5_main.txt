Headline: Elm Clinic Missed Both 2024 Imaging Deadlines, Bulletin States

In this compliance bulletin, MedData says Elm Clinic failed to submit both its 2024 radiology safety addendum and contrast-event nil report before the applicable deadlines. The bulletin presents the clinic as an example of a provider that did not complete two expected filings within the required window for the 2024 cycle.

According to the bulletin's narrative, the lapse covered both the radiology safety addendum and the contrast-event nil report rather than a single isolated item. The write-up describes the matter in categorical terms and frames the clinic's filing posture as non-timely for each of the two obligations.

The desk summary is written in a firm compliance-reporting style, treating the missed timing as settled and not provisional. It groups Elm Clinic with providers cited for delayed action and states that the deadlines applicable to the 2024 submissions were not met for either item.

MedData's publication does not cast the issue as partial completion or a mixed result. Its stated position is straightforward: Elm Clinic did not submit the two listed 2024 filings before their deadlines.
