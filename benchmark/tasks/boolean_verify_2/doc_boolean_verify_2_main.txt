Issue: Midyear provider filing performance

Briar Clinic failed to submit both its 2024 device calibration attestation and sterile-field variance report before the applicable deadlines.

The bulletin is being circulated to regional compliance managers as part of the current provider-performance digest. In this issue, the desk highlights filing timeliness concerns requiring closer attention during the 2024 cycle. Briar Clinic is listed as having missed the required timing on both of the named submissions, and managers are asked to account for that outcome in routine provider oversight planning.

The brief uses the standard bulletin format for concise status statements rather than detailed transaction histories. Its purpose is to give supervisory staff a quick reading of where filing execution fell short and which organizations should be treated as priorities for follow-up attention. For Briar Clinic, the desk characterization is unequivocal: neither of the two specified 2024 submissions was made before its applicable deadline.
