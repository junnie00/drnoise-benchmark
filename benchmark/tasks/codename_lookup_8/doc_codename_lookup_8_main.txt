Headline: Borealis Lab cold-chamber R-87 work identified as Grey Arrow

FieldData Network reports that the Borealis Lab experiment using R-87 in a 4 degrees C chamber carried the codename Grey Arrow.

The report, circulated on the network’s internal science wire, describes the project in confident terms and presents the codename as settled. Editors on the briefing desk received the item as part of a routine roundup of laboratory activity notes and preserved the wording used in the incoming transmission. In the version moved for subscribers, the laboratory, reagent class, chamber condition, and codename are stated together without qualification.

No alternate codename is given anywhere in the brief, and the dispatch does not frame the identification as tentative. Instead, it treats Grey Arrow as the standing name attached to the Borealis Lab experiment described by those operating conditions. That naming appears in the body of the network copy and is echoed in the headline language prepared for distribution.
