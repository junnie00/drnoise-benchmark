Headline: Grove Clinic missed both 2024 filing deadlines, bulletin states

MedData's compliance bulletin says Grove Clinic failed to submit both its 2024 pharmacy delegation log and temperature excursion nil report before the applicable deadlines. The bulletin frames the matter as a dual lapse in the 2024 clinic return cycle and presents the clinic's position as non-timely on each of the two scheduled submissions.

According to the bulletin notice, the issue concerns the annual pharmacy delegation log and the temperature excursion nil report expected from Grove Clinic during 2024. In the bulletin's summary language, neither filing is treated as having arrived within the window set for acceptance, and the clinic is described as missing the required dates for both items.

The notice appears in the standard compliance-bulletin style used for concise operational updates, with a direct statement of the clinic's filing position rather than a narrative case history. No broader provider comparison is offered in the bulletin text; it simply characterizes Grove Clinic as not having submitted the two named records before their applicable deadlines.
