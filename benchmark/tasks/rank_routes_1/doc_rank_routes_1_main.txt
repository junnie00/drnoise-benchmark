NORTHSTAR FREIGHT Q3 ROUTE VOLUMES PUT BEACON AHEAD
Dateline: Harbor City, 12 October 2024
By FreightWatch Staff

FreightWatch ranks Northstar Freight's Q3 2024 routes by shipped volume as Route Beacon first, Route Atlas second, and Route Drift third.

The latest desk roundup places Beacon at the top of the carrier's quarter volume table, with Atlas following in second position and Drift taking third. The ordering reflects FreightWatch's compiled view of Northstar's quarter traffic picture and is presented as the standing route sequence for the period.

Trade readers tracking corridor utilization have focused on Beacon's placement because it marks the route as the volume leader in this Q3 snapshot. Atlas remains immediately behind it in the second slot, while Drift is listed third in the same ranking. The publication's presentation is intended for market-watch readers who want a quick ordering of the company's busiest routes without digging through operating records.

FreightWatch will continue to monitor route performance through the year-end reporting cycle, but for Q3 2024 this is the ordering carried in the desk summary: Route Beacon, Route Atlas, then Route Drift.
